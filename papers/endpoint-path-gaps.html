Relative-Entropy Endpoint–Path Gaps as a Diagnostic of RG Coarse-Graining Failure

Author: Zihe Zhao
PDF: Relative_Entropy_Endpoint_Path_Gaps_as_a_Diagnostic_of_RG_Coarse_Graining_Failure.pdf
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18414030
Repo: endpoint-path-gaps-qed

What I did (machine-first)

  • I fixed the analysis setting as a viewpoint object V (interface/protocol, scheme/regulator, model class + reconstruction, divergence, aggregation, thresholds).
  • I defined the strict endpoint–path decomposition ∆path = ∆pair + Γpath with Γpath ≥ 0, and used Γpath=0 as the sharp endpoint sufficiency criterion under the fixed V.
  • I constructed a computable Route-II geometric proxy Γgeo := LII − dend under explicit validity conditions (treating Γgeo↔Γpath as calibration, not identity).

Minimal objects (anchors)

V (viewpoint object)
Locks what is observed and how comparisons are licensed; all quantities are V-conditioned.
∆path, ∆pair, Γpath
Strict bridge objects with exact decomposition; Γpath=0 ⇔ endpoint sufficiency under V.
∆I
Route-I endpoint divergence (interface-aggregated endpoint discrepancy).
Ψ, LII, dend, Γgeo
Route-II induced geometric objects; Γgeo defined only within declared validity conditions.
