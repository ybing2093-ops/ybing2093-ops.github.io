From Endpoint Sufficiency to Structural Gaps

Author: Zihe Zhao
PDF: From_Endpoint_Sufficiency_to_Structural_Gaps.pdf
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19081150
Project: Structural Gaps — Canonical Entry

What I did (machine-first)

  • I clarified the shared framework-level problem behind endpoint-path gaps, declared structural residuals, and fixed-slice fiber diagnostics.
  • I made explicit that coarse descriptions are reportable only after visibility, admissibility, comparability, reconstruction, and negligibility clauses are locked by a declared viewpoint/package.
  • I separated the diagnostic roles of ψ and ∆: within-level tension versus cross-level residual structure.
  • I organized the output space into licensed near-zero reports, non-negligible residuals, package-locked statements, and non-licensed/undefined outcomes.

Minimal objects (anchors)

V / declared package
Locks the interface, admissible objects, reconstruction discipline, discrepancy rule, and reporting resolution under which a cross-level statement becomes reportable.
ψ
Framework-level marker for within-level structural tension under a fixed perspective and declared package.
∆
Framework-level carrier of cross-level residual structure; not a generic error term and not interchangeable with slice-local residuals by default.
Endpoint sufficiency
A theorem-level or package-licensed claim, not something implied by endpoint agreement alone.
Non-licensed output
The correct report state when admissibility, comparability, reconstruction, or transport clauses are missing.
