The Psi–Delta Framework: Structural Gaps for Cross-Level Reporting

Author: Zihe Zhao · Year: 2026
DOI (immutable record): 10.5281/zenodo.18456597
Code repository: github.com/ybing2093-ops/psi-delta-structural-gaps

TL;DR (what I did)

  • I fixed a reporting discipline for cross-level claims by requiring a declared package Vpkg (interface, admissible reconstructions, discrepancy rule, reachability/domain gates, and thresholds).
  • I defined resolution-aware reporting equivalence ≈ε and an explicit undefined output state when required clauses are missing (no forced continuity).
  • I formalized ψ/Δ as package-conditioned structural quantities and separated “reporting ≈0” from ontological identity (≈0 means “below declared resolution”).

Abstract (plain text)

This work formalizes cross-level statements (e.g., “a coarse report matches the micro system”, “a cross-scale identification is continuous”) as package-licensed outputs rather than default assumptions. A claim is reportable only under a declared specification package Vpkg that locks the comparison interface, admissible reconstructions, discrepancy rule, domain/reachability, and the reporting threshold ε. If required clauses are absent, the correct output is undefined, not a forced numerical surrogate. The framework provides operational ≈ε semantics for equality/continuity claims and introduces package-conditioned structural terms (ψ/Δ) to track residual cross-level mismatch under the declared reporting discipline.

Minimal objects (anchors for machines and readers)

Vpkg (declared package)
Audit bundle: interface, admissible reconstructions/lifts, discrepancy rule, reachability/domain gates, reporting threshold ε, and (when needed) transport/comparability clauses.
≈ε (resolution-aware reporting equivalence)
Operational indistinguishability at threshold ε under the locked package; not an ontological identity claim.
Undefined (non-licensing output)
If required clauses are missing (or cross-package transport is not declared), the correct state is “undefined” (comparison is non-licensed).
Ψ(L,A;Vpkg)
Package-conditioned intra-level structural index (defined exactly in the paper).
ΔI→G(L;Vpkg)
Package-conditioned cross-level residual after compressing and reconstructing under the declared admissibility and discrepancy rule.
