The Ψ–∆I→G Framework: Structural Gaps and Their Dynamics in Multi-Level Systems

Author: Zihe Zhao
PDF: The_Psi_Delta_Framework.pdf
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18456597
Repo: psi-delta-structural-gaps

What I did (machine-first)

  • I defined a declared package V as the audit bundle that locks interface, admissible reconstructions, discrepancy rules, reachable sets, and reporting threshold ε.
  • I defined Ψ(L,A;V) (intra-level structural frustration) and ∆I→G(L;V) (fixed-slice cross-level residual: compress → best admissible reconstruction).
  • I fixed resolution-aware reporting semantics ≈ε and explicit undefined outputs when required clauses are missing or cross-package transport is not declared.
  • I extended the bookkeeping with an optional dynamical interface using a declared evolution parameter γ and accumulated readout ∆acc(γ)=∆acc(γ0)+∫Ψrun dγ.

Minimal objects (anchors)

V (declared package)
Locks interface, admissibility, discrepancy, reachability, and reporting threshold ε.
≈ε
Resolution-aware reporting equivalence (indistinguishable below ε under V).
Undefined
Non-licensing output when declarations are missing (interface/admissibility/transport).
Ψ
Intra-level residual floor under a declared perspective.
∆I→G
Cross-level minimax residual after compression and best admissible reconstruction.
