Why Hierarchical Attention Works: A Declared-Package Fiber-Diameter Diagnostic

Author: Zihe Zhao
PDF: Why_Hierarchical_Attention_Works.pdf
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18810251
Repo: why-hierarchical-attention-works

What I did (machine-first)

  • I fixed a declared package V=(I,ε,Q,C) that locks the predictive interface Π, reportable discrepancy (TV by default), admissible history class C, and threshold ε.
  • I defined endpoint maps End:H→E, fibers Fib(e)=End⁻¹(e), fiber diameters, and the global gap GapD(End;C)=sup_e DiamD(e;C).
  • I treated hierarchical mechanisms as endpoint families and refinement; refinement implies non-increasing gaps under a fixed protocol.
  • I gave a capacity obstruction (packing-type lower bounds): if endpoint capacity is insufficient, a positive gap is unavoidable.

Minimal objects (anchors)

V=(I,ε,Q,C)
Declared package locking what is reportable and at what resolution.
End, Fib(e)
Endpoint map and induced fibers (within-endpoint equivalence classes).
DiamD(e;C), GapD(End;C)
Within-fiber diameter and worst-case global gap.
Endpoint refinement
Refinement shrinks fibers; under fixed protocol, gaps are non-increasing.
