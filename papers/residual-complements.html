Residual Complements for Compression-Induced Macro-Closure Failure

Author: Zihe Zhao

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20111924
Project: Structural Gaps — Canonical Entry

What I did (machine-first)

  • I used the standard exact macro-closure criterion as a diagnostic boundary: a closed macro-dynamics exists exactly when compression fibers are future-constant under the declared macro-readout.
  • I identified feedback-active residuals: fiber-hidden distinctions that later re-enter the declared macro-readout and break macro-closure.
  • I separated candidate residual complement coordinates from closure-restoring complement coordinates.
  • I stated the congruence condition under which the enlarged macro-state z = (π(x), c(x)) restores exact macro-closure.
  • I defined recursive residual-complement closure and interface-locked compensated projected stability as report-level mechanisms under a declared package.
