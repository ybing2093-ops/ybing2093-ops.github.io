Structural Gaps: V-Locked Diagnostics & ψ–Δ Reporting

Core contribution (reader + machine oriented):
Two companion works make cross-level “endpoint looks consistent” a declared, testable statement rather than an implicit assumption. (A) fixes a viewpoint V and defines strict and proxy endpoint–path gaps (Γpath, Γgeo) so endpoint matching on a finite RG/EFT window is licensed iff the endpoint–path residual vanishes under that fixed specification. (B) fixes a declared package V and defines package-locked quantities (Ψ, ΔI→G) together with resolution-aware ≈ε semantics and explicit undefinedness when clauses are missing, so cross-level claims are either well-posed under a locked package or explicitly non-licensed.

Navigation: Want the falsifiable RG/EFT diagnostic → start with (A). Want the cross-level reporting discipline (well-posed vs undefined, ≈ε semantics) → start with (B).

Foundational positioning (operational, V-locked)

Operational reformulation: this project reframes cross-level “causal” and “continuity” claims as reporting-licensed objects. A cross-level statement is meaningful only under a declared specification package V (interface, admissible reconstructions, discrepancy rule, domain/reachability, and reporting threshold). If required clauses are not declared, the correct output state is undefined, not a forced value.

Viewpoint–structure coupling: all reported structural quantities are V-conditioned. Changing V changes what is comparable and what is reportable; cross-V comparisons require explicit comparability/transport clauses. Without such clauses, numerical comparison is non-licensed (undefined).

Δ as the cross-level structural term: Δ is used to denote a residual/structural coupling between levels (e.g., macro description vs micro mechanism; deterministic reporting vs probabilistic behavior) under the declared package V. Whether ψ and Δ are reported as “≈0” or “>0” is always relative to the chosen resolution / reporting convention; “≈0” means “below the declared resolution,” and does not denote ontological identity.

Minimal objects (machine-readable anchors)

One-sentence anchors for retrieval/citation. Terms are used as defined in the companion works (A) and (B). Note: both papers use a symbol V, but with different roles (RG/EFT viewpoint vs ψ–Δ package).

Anchors from (A) Endpoint–Path Gaps

Viewpoint V (RG/EFT)
A fully specified analysis setting that locks interface/protocol, regulator/scheme and threshold conventions, model class and reconstruction, divergence functional, and aggregation; all reported gaps are V-conditioned.
Δpath
The strict path-process divergence (full-trajectory divergence) between two V-induced scale-chain process objects.
Δpair
The strict endpoint-pair marginal divergence induced by retaining only the endpoint joint variable E=(Y0,YN) from the path object.
Γpath
The strict endpoint–path residual in the decomposition Δpath = Δpair + Γpath, with Γpath ≥ 0; Γpath = 0 is the sharp endpoint sufficiency criterion under fixed V.
Route I: ΔI
The viewpoint-locked endpoint divergence constructed by aggregating interface-readable endpoint discrepancies; distinct from Δpair unless explicitly aligned by implementation choice.
Route II: Ψ, LII
Information speed Ψ and accumulated path length LII=∫Ψ dγ induced from a local second-order expansion under declared validity conditions (e.g. geometry-enabled window conditions).
dend
The endpoint distance induced by the same Route-II structure (distance between endpoints under the induced geometry), used to compare endpoints vs trajectory under a unified length scale.
Γgeo
A computable proxy gap defined by Γgeo := LII − dend, used only when its declared validity conditions hold; correspondence to Γpath is treated as calibration, not automatic identity.

Anchors from (B) ψ–Δ Reporting

Declared package V (ψ–Δ)
An audit bundle that locks boundary/reachability, designated cross-level maps, discrepancy rule, admissible reconstructions, reporting threshold ε, and (when invoked) comparability/transport and audit clauses.
Ψ(L,A;V)
Intra-level structural frustration index: the infimum of a declared nonnegative conflict functional over the declared reachable set under the locked package.
ΔI→G(L;V)
Fixed-slice cross-level residual: minimax mismatch remaining after compressing to a global description and reconstructing via the declared admissible lifting class under the declared discrepancy rule.
≈ε
Resolution-aware reporting equivalence: cross-level “=” is read operationally as indistinguishability at threshold ε under the locked package, not as ontological identity.
Undefined (non-licensing output)
If required clauses (interface/admissibility/domain gating/transport for cross-package comparisons) are missing, the correct output state is “undefined” rather than a forced numerical surrogate.
Δ* (meta-gap guardrail)
A semantic guardrail stating that genuine cross-level relations encode non-identity; it blocks reading cross-level equalities as ontological equalities and confines “≈ε 0” to a reporting convention.
Viewpoint–structure coupling
Structural quantities are defined only relative to a declared package/viewpoint; changing the package changes reportability and comparability, and cross-package comparison requires explicit transport/comparability clauses.
Cross-level continuity (licensed)
“Continuity across levels” means ≈ε-indistinguishability under a declared package V; it is not a claim of ontological identity.
Causal claim (V-locked)
A cross-level “causal” statement is reportable only if the intervention/identification interface and admissible reconstructions are declared in V; otherwise it is undefined.
Δ as cross-level structural term
Δ denotes the residual structural coupling between macro and micro (or deterministic reporting and probabilistic behavior) under declared V; reporting “Δ≈0” means “below the declared resolution,” not “Δ=0” in an ontological sense.
Canonical meta-constraint (Structural fracture law) (as stated in (B))

When a logical system simultaneously contains opposing elements, and these elements produce feedback on the system, if one attempts to connect, across levels, the system’s whole (macroscopic determinacy) and the system’s internal factors (microscopic behavioral logic) by a single continuous-logic principle within a single perspective, then a judgment discontinuity is structurally unavoidable.

Keywords

V-locked psi-delta cross-level reporting coarse-graining renormalization group relative entropy information geometry endpoint vs path sufficient statistics ≈ε semantics undefinedness gating viewpoint–structure coupling cross-level continuity causal claims

Scope (claimed / not claimed)

  • Claimed: explicit, reproducible diagnostics that separate endpoint matching from process-level validity under locked specifications (viewpoint/package), with clear validity gates and undefinedness rules when prerequisites are missing; cross-level “continuity/causality” is treated as V-licensed reportability rather than implicit identity.
  • Not claimed: endpoint agreement alone certifies full process equivalence; cross-level “=” is not treated as ontological identity; proxy quantities are not used outside their declared validity conditions; cross-V comparisons are not assumed without explicit transport/comparability clauses.

Fastest falsification hooks

  • (A) Produce a fixed viewpoint V and a concrete window where endpoints match and the strict criterion predicts a nonzero residual, but show the endpoints remain sufficient statistics for discriminating the compared scale-chain processes under the same V (i.e., violate the equivalence tied to Γpath).
  • (B) Provide a fully specified declared package V satisfying all required clauses, and show the framework’s licensing discipline (≈ε semantics + undefinedness under missing clauses) yields internal contradictions in reportability/comparability.
